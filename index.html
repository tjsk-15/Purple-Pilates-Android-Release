How to install

1

Tap the purple download button above

Your phone will start downloading the app. You may see a small notification at the top of your screen when it's done.

2

Open the file when it finishes downloading

Tap the notification, or open your Downloads folder and tap the file that ends in .apk.

3

If your phone shows a warning, tap "Open file"

Android shows this warning for every app that isn't from the Play Store yet. This is normal — your phone just wants to double-check.

This file may be harmful

Opening files from someone you don't know can lead to scams or put your device's safety at risk.

Cancel
Open file

↑ Tap "Open file" — that's the one you want.

Don't worry — it's safe. The warning appears because we haven't finished listing on the Play Store yet. After installing, you can open the app like any other.

4

Tap "Install" and you're all set ✨

Once installed, you'll find Purple Pilates with your other apps. Open it and sign in to start your first class.

Common questions

Is it really safe?
Yes. The warning is Android's standard message for any app installed outside the Play Store — it shows up even for big, trusted apps when they're shared this way. Our app is the same one that will appear on the Play Store in about two weeks.
I can't find the downloaded file. Where is it?
Open the Files or My Files app on your phone, then tap Downloads. You're looking for a file with a name ending in .apk.
My phone says "for your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps." What do I do?
Tap Settings on that screen, then turn on "Allow from this source". Go back and tap Install again. You only have to do this once.
Can I install this on an iPhone?
Not yet — this file is only for Android phones. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.
Will the Play Store version replace this one?
Yes. When the app is approved on the Play Store, you'll be able to update it from there without losing anything.
