How to install

1

Tap the green "Get it on Google Play" button above

It will open the Purple Pilates listing in the Google Play Store on your phone.

2

Look for the "Early Access" section

On the Play Store page, scroll down a little. You'll see a section that says "Early access" with a green Install button right next to it.

Heads up: The app is in Early Access while we finish testing, so you won't see the usual big "Install" button at the top — it lives under the Early Access section instead.

3

Tap "Install" and you're all set ✨

The app will download and install automatically. Once it's done, open Purple Pilates and sign in to start your first class.

Common questions

What is "Early Access"?
Early Access is Google Play's way of letting you try a new app before its full public launch. It's the same official app — you're just getting it a bit earlier than everyone else. It installs and updates from the Play Store like any other app.
I don't see an Install button — only "Join Early Access". What do I do?
That's the right one! Tap Join Early Access (or Install in the Early Access section). Google may take a minute to enroll you, then the download will start.
The Play Store says the app isn't available in my country / on my device.
Make sure you're signed in to the Google account you usually use, and that your phone is running Android 8 or newer. If you're still stuck, message us on WhatsApp below and we'll sort it out.
Can I install this on an iPhone?
Not yet — Purple Pilates is currently Android only. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.
Will I keep getting updates after Early Access ends?
Yes. When the app graduates to a regular Play Store release, your phone will keep updating it automatically. You won't lose any data or your account.
